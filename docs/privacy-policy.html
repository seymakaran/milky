PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: 22 December 2025

This Privacy Policy is prepared by ODEgames and applies to all of our mobile applications published on Google Play and the Apple App Store.

Protecting children’s privacy is extremely important to us. Our applications are designed specifically for children and comply with all applicable child privacy laws and platform policies.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

Our applications do not collect, request, store, or process any personal information from children or parents.

We do not collect any of the following:

  • Names or nicknames
  • Email addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Precise or approximate location data
  • Photos, videos, or audio recordings
  • User accounts, login credentials, or identifiers

No registration, login, or account creation is required to use our applications.

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

Our applications do not collect any automatically generated data.

Specifically, we do not use:

  • Analytics or usage tracking tools
  • Crash reporting services
  • Third-party SDKs that collect data
  • Cookies or similar tracking technologies

2. Advertising

Our applications do not display advertisements of any kind.

There are:

  • No banner ads
  • No interstitial ads
  • No rewarded ads
  • No third-party advertising networks

3. In-App Purchases

Our applications include a single, one-time in-app purchase.

This purchase permanently unlocks all content and features in the app.

  • No subscriptions
  • No recurring payments
  • No time-limited access

All payments are securely processed by:

  • Google Play (for Android devices)
  • Apple App Store (for iOS devices)

ODEgames does not receive, store, or have access to any payment or billing information.

4. Data Sharing

Because our applications do not collect any personal or usage data, no data is shared with third parties.

5. Children’s Privacy

Our applications are intended for children and comply with:

  • Google Play Families Policy
  • Apple App Store Kids Category requirements
  • COPPA (Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act)

We knowingly do not collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.

6. Data Security

Since no personal or device data is collected or stored, there is no risk of user data exposure through our applications.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect legal or platform policy changes.

Any updates will be published on this page with a revised “Last Updated” date.
